Daybook Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Daybook is a personal planner and study-management app. This policy describes information handled by the Daybook Android app and website.

Information stored on your device

Schedules, tasks, study records, habits, budget entries, preferences, reminders and backup data are stored locally in the app's device/browser storage. Daybook does not provide an account, cloud synchronization, advertising SDK, or analytics service. The developer does not receive this locally stored content through Daybook.

Exported backups are files you choose to create and share. They may contain personal schedule, academic, habit and finance details. Store them securely and delete them when no longer needed. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may erase local records.

Prayer-time service

When prayer times are requested and the device is online, Daybook sends the selected date and prayer-time calculation preferences, including the configured latitude and longitude, to the AlAdhan API (api.aladhan.com) to calculate prayer times. The app caches prayer timings locally for offline use. Daybook does not request device GPS location for this feature; location values are configured in the app. AlAdhan is a third-party service and its own privacy practices apply to requests it receives.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Daybook uses Android notification functionality to schedule notifications on your device. Notification permission and, on supported Android versions, exact-alarm settings are controlled by Android. Reminder titles and scheduled times are used locally to schedule the notifications.

Sharing and disclosure

Daybook does not sell personal information or send your planner records to the developer. Information may leave the device only when you deliberately export/share a backup or when the app requests prayer-time data from the third-party AlAdhan service as described above.

Children

Daybook is a general productivity tool and is not designed specifically for children under 13.

Security and retention

Data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Device security, backups and access controls are your responsibility. No method of local or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the Daybook project support page.
